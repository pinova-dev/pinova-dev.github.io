Privacy Policy for PiNova

Last Updated: October 28, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to PiNova. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our application within the Pi Network ecosystem.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to provide our services:

  • Pi Network Username & User ID: Used solely for authentication and identifying your account within the app.
  • App Usage Data: Anonymous data about how you interact with the app to improve user experience.
  • E-commerce Data: Transaction history and purchase data for order fulfillment.
  • AI Processing Data: Content you create using our AI tools (processed securely).

We do NOT collect your Pi Wallet passphrase, private keys, or any sensitive financial information.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively to:

  • Authenticate your identity via the Pi SDK.
  • Process legitimate Pi Network transactions (payments/rewards) initiated by you.
  • Provide AI-powered content creation and cybersecurity services.
  • Improve and maintain the functionality of PiNova.
  • Facilitate e-commerce transactions between buyers and sellers.

4. AI Data Processing

Our AI tools process data locally or on secure servers to provide personalized recommendations and content generation. We do not store your personal content without explicit consent.

5. Cybersecurity Measures

We employ industry-standard encryption, multi-factor authentication, and real-time monitoring to protect your data and transactions. Your security is our top priority.

6. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with any third parties, except as required to facilitate Pi Network blockchain transactions or comply with legal obligations.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your data. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure. You are responsible for keeping your Pi Wallet credentials safe.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Opt-out of non-essential data processing

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
